“66 and 99”

When someone talks in a story, we put their words inside speech marks. They look like a tiny 66 at the start and a tiny 99 at the end!

Tap the first word the puppet says.

Two steps

Do this when someone talks.

1

Find the words the person says.

Ben said, I go to the park on Sunday.

2

Put “ before those words, and ” after them.

Ben said, “I go to the park on Sunday.”

“66 opens
”99 closes

The end mark goes inside

The full stop, question mark or exclamation mark is part of what is said. So it goes before the 99.

Mum said, “It is late”.

Mum said, “It is late.”

And only the words that are said go inside. Mum said stays outside!

Telling, asking or shouting?

Choose the end mark, just like in Lesson 1.

said … .

Telling something? Use a full stop.

Jane said, “I like flowers.”

asked … ?

Asking something? Use a question mark.

Tom asked, “Can I play?”

shouted … !

A big feeling or a shout? Use an exclamation mark.

Ali shouted, “Watch out!”

Two little helpers

A comma after "said"

Put a comma after said or asked, before the 66.

Ben said, “Hello!”

A capital to start

The first word inside the speech marks gets a capital letter.

Mei said, “We can play now.”
