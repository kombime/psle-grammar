Mirror, Mirror

When you do something to yourself, the word bounces back like a mirror: I see myself!

🙋I
🙋?

Three kinds of words

Words for yourself, for people, and for things.

🪞

Did it to yourself? Use a mirror word. It ends in -self or -selves.

I see myself · they help themselves

👥

Which people? One person you don't know: someone. All the people: everyone. Not one person: no one.

📦

Which things? something, everything, nothing work the same way.

Watch out!

Words that look right, but aren't.

No "hisself" or "theirselves"

These words don't exist. The mirror uses him and them.

hisself → himself

theirselves → themselves

ourself → ourselves (we are more than one)

No one is two words

Someone and everyone are one word. No one is two!

noone → no one

The word families

Find the right word for each person.

Mirror words: one person

Add -self.

  • I → myself
  • you → yourself
  • he → himself
  • she → herself
  • it → itself

By myself means on my own, with no help: I did it by myself!

Mirror words: more than one

More than one? Use -selves, just like leaf → leaves!

  • we → ourselves
  • they → themselves

Everyone is…

Everyone means lots of people, but it works like he.

Say "is" and "has"

  • He is → everyone is
  • He has → no one has
  • He likes → everyone likes

Everyone are ready.
Everyone is ready.

The same for the others

  • Someone is at the door.
  • No one has the key.
  • Everything is ready.
