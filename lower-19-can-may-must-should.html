The Helper Heroes

Four helper heroes help the doing word. Each one has its own power. Read the mission, then send the right hero!

MISSION

Two steps

Pick the hero, then add the doing word.

1

What do you mean? Able to, asking politely, a rule, or a good idea?

can · may · must · should

2

Add the plain doing word. No -s, no -ed, no -ing, no "to".

can swim · must go

Watch out!

Heroes travel light.

The doing word stays plain

After a hero, use the plain doing word.

She can swims. → She can swim.

You must to go. → You must go.

Heroes never take -s

Even with he, she or one name!

He cans run. → He can run.

She shoulds rest. → She should rest.

Meet the heroes

Each hero has a badge. Look for the badges in the quiz!

💪 can

💪 able to

Birds can fly.

I can ride a bike.

🙋 may

🙋 asking politely

May I go to the toilet, please?

May I have some water?

📏 must

📏 a rule

You must wear a seat belt.

We must stop at the red man.

💡 should

💡 a good idea

You should drink water.

You should take an umbrella.

More hero powers

Two more things to know.

📏 must not

A "don't" rule uses must not.

NO EATING 🚫🍔 → You must not eat here.

DO NOT TOUCH → You must not touch it.

🔍 Badge clues

In the quiz, a badge in brackets tells you what the sentence means: (💡 a good idea). Pick the hero with that badge!
