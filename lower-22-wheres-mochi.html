Where's Mochi?

Mochi the kitten loves to hide! Look at the picture, then pick the word that says where Mochi is.

Look, then say where

Place words tell us where something is.

👀

Look at the thing Mochi is near: the box, the table, the tree.

📍

Where is Mochi? Inside? On top? Below? Pick the place word.

Mochi is in the box. 📦

Watch out!

in or into?

Into is for moving inside. If something is just sitting there, use in.

Mochi jumped into the box. ➡️📦

Now Mochi is in the box. 📦

The milk is into the fridge. → The milk is in the fridge.

The opposite of into is out of: Mochi climbed out of the box.

Mochi's hiding places

Three groups of place words.

in · on · under

📦🐱in the box
🐱
🟫on the table
🟫
🐱under the table

The picture is on the wall. The fish are in the water.

behind · in front of

Behind: at the back, maybe hidden. In front of: you see it first.

Mochi is hiding behind the tree. 🌳

Mochi is sitting in front of the tree.

First in the queue? Everyone is behind you!

next to · between

Next to: beside one thing. Between: one thing on each side.

📦🐱 Mochi is next to the box.

🐶🐱🐶 Mochi is between the dogs.

B comes between A and C.

With

"With" doesn't tell where. It has two jobs.

👫 Together

  • I went to the zoo with my family.
  • Mochi plays with a ball.

✂️ Using, or having

  • I eat rice with a spoon.
  • Dad cut it with scissors.
  • the girl with long hair
