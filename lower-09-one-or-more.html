Three steps

How do you make a word mean more than one?

1

One or more? Words like two, three, many and a lot of mean more than one. A or one means just one: the word stays the same.

a cat → two cats

2

Look at the end of the word. The end tells you which door to use.

fox · baby · leaf

3

Is it a surprise word? A few words don't use a door at all. Learn them by heart!

child → children · sheep → sheep

Watch out!

Two mistakes the machine sees a lot.

Hissing ends need -es

Say it out loud: box-es. You can hear the extra bit!

two boxs → two boxes

two watchs → two watches

boy, toy, key: just add -s

Remember the vowels: aeiou. If a vowel sits next to the y, the y stays. Just add -s!

toies → toys  (o is a vowel)

monkies → monkeys  (e is a vowel)

The four doors

Look at the end of the word, then pick the door.

+ s

Most words just add -s. Words that end in e just add -s too.

  • cat → cats
  • book → books
  • plate → plates
  • kite → kites

+ es

Ends in s, ss, x, ch or sh? Add -es.

  • bus → buses
  • class → classes
  • box → boxes
  • watch → watches
  • dish → dishes

y → ies

Ends in y, with no vowel before it? Change y to i, then add -es.

  • baby → babies
  • cherry → cherries
  • story → stories
  • party → parties

f → ves

Ends in f or fe? Change it to v, then add -es.

  • leaf → leaves
  • knife → knives
  • calf 🐄 → calves
  • thief → thieves

🎁 Surprise words

These words don't use a door. Learn them by heart!

They change inside

  • child → children
  • man → men
  • woman → women
  • foot → feet
  • tooth → teeth
  • mouse → mice
  • goose → geese

They already mean more than one, so never add -s: childrens, mices.

They stay the same

  • one sheep → two sheep 🐑🐑
  • one deer → three deer 🦌🦌🦌

No -s at all: sheeps, deers.
