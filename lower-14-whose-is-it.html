Three ways to say whose

Ask: who is the owner?

1

One owner? Add 's.

Ravi's bag · the cat's bowl

2

Many owners, ending in s? Just add ' after the s.

the ducks' pond 🦆🦆

3

No name? Use an owner word: my bag, or the bag is mine.

Watch out!

Two mistakes with the little mark '.

Don't forget it on names

Without the mark, "Ravis" sounds like many Ravis!

Ravis bag → Ravi's bag

Alis eraser → Ali's eraser

Owner words never have one

yours, hers, ours, theirs and mine have no ' at all.

your's → yours · her's → hers

their's → theirs · mine's → mine

The labels

Three kinds of label for the lost things.

One owner: 's

Siti's pencil ✏️

Dad's hat 🎩

the dog's bowl 🐶

Many owners: s'

The word already ends in s, so just add '.

the ducks' pond 🦆🦆🦆

the girls' room 👧👧

the cats' toys 🐱🐱

Surprise owners

Remember the surprise words from Lesson 9?

No s at the end? Add 's

  • children → the children's playground
  • men → the men's toilet
  • women → the women's team

These mean many people, but they don't end in s. So they get 's.

Many teachers, one room

Lots of teachers share one staff room. Teachers ends in s, so: the teachers' room.

One teacher's bag: the teacher's bag.
