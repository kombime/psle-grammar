Bridge Builders

Two little sentences sit on two islands. A joining word is a bridge between them. Pick the right bridge to join them into one sentence!

?

Read both islands

Then ask: how do they go together?

1

Read both parts. What does each part say?

2

Pick the bridge that shows how they go together: more, opposite, choice, why, or what next.

and · but · or · because · so

Watch out!

Because and so face opposite ways.

Because points back to the reason. So points forward to what happened next.

🌧️ It was raining so we stayed indoors. ➡️

🏠 We stayed indoors because it was raining. ⬅️

It was raining because we stayed indoors.

Five bridges

Each bridge does a different job.

and

➕ adds more

I have a cat and a dog.

but

↔️ the opposite, or a surprise

I like apples but I don't like pears.

or

🤔 a choice

Do you want tea or Milo?

because

⬅️ tells why

I wore a jacket because it was cold.

so

➡️ what happened next

I was hungry so I ate a sandwich.

Joining two sentences

Two short sentences can become one.

Take out the full stop

Mei was tired. She went to bed.

→ Mei was tired so she went to bed.

The full stop goes, the bridge goes in, and "She" becomes "she".

"Or else"

Or can also warn what will happen if you don't do something.

Hurry up or you will miss the bus! 🚌
