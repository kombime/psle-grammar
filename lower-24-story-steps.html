Story Steps

Let's make kaya toast! Tap the steps in the right order. The step words First, Next, Then and Finally pop in front!

    Think: what happens first?

    Step words tell the reader the order.

    1

    Put the steps in order. Ask: what has to happen before the next thing can happen?

    🧦 socks before 👟 shoes

    2

    Add the step words. First at the start, next, then, after that in the middle, finally at the end.

    🍞First,toast the bread
    🟢Next,spread the kaya
    🧈Thenadd butter
    😋Finally,eat it!

    Watch out!

    The words must match the order.

    Finally is only for the last step. First is only for the first step.

    Finally, I woke up. Then I had breakfast.

    First, I woke up. Then I had breakfast.

    Beginning, middle, end

    Every set of steps has three parts.

    The start: First

    First, I took out the bread.

    The middle: Next, Then, After that

    Use any of these. They all mean "the step after".

    Next, I put it in the toaster.

    Then I spread the kaya.

    After that, I added butter.

    The end: Finally

    Finally, I ate my kaya toast! 😋

    Checking a whole story

    Read it like a film. Does it make sense?

    Does it make sense?

    Picture each step. Could it really happen in that order?

    First, I ate the cake. Then I baked it.

    First, I baked the cake. Then I ate it.

    Look for the step out of place

    One step in the wrong place spoils the story.

    wrap the gift 🎁 → tie a ribbon 🎀 → give it to Mum → buy the gift

    You must buy it first!
