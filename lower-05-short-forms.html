Hug Words

Sometimes two words give each other a big hug. They squeeze so tight that some letters pop out! The little mark ' shows where they were.

Pick two words, then tap Hug!

Two steps

How to make a hug word.

1

Push the two words together.

do not → donot

2

Take out the squeezed letters. Put ' in their place.

donot → don't

Put ' where the letters left

The ' does not go between the two words. It goes where the missing letters were.

do'nt → don't

is'nt → isn't

And don't forget it! dont → don't

Hug word families

Each family loses the same letters.

n't = not

The o in "not" pops out.

  • isn't is not
  • aren't are not
  • don't do not
  • doesn't does not
  • didn't did not
  • shouldn't should not
  • wasn't was not
  • haven't have not

'll = will

The wi in "will" pops out.

  • I'll I will
  • you'll you will
  • he'll he will
  • she'll she will
  • we'll we will
  • they'll they will

's 'm 're = is, am, are

The first letter of "is", "am" or "are" pops out.

  • he's he is
  • she's she is
  • it's it is
  • I'm I am
  • you're you are
  • we're we are
  • they're they are

Two odd ones

These two hug words are a little different.

won't 🙅

will not does not become "willn't". It becomes won't!

I won't tell anyone.

can't 🙌

cannot is one word. Squeeze out the no: can't.

Fish can't fly.
