Stop, Ask or Wow?

A sentence is like a little car. At the end of the road, it must stop. You choose the sign!

Read the sentence. Then tap a sign.

Two steps

Do this at the end of every sentence.

1

Read the sentence out loud.

2

Ask yourself: is it telling, asking or saying wow?

Telling → .   Asking → ?   Wow → !

Watch out for asking words

These words often start a question:

  • Who
  • What
  • Where
  • When
  • Why
  • How
  • Can
  • Do
  • Is
  • Are
  • Will

Where is my bag.

Where is my bag?

If it asks something, it needs a question mark.

The three signs

Each kind of sentence has its own sign.

Stop: full stop .

For a telling sentence.

My cat is sleeping.

We play at recess.

Ask: question mark ?

For an asking sentence.

Do you like ice cream?

Where is Mum?

Wow: exclamation mark !

For a big feeling: surprise, joy, fear or a shout.

Wow, a rainbow!
