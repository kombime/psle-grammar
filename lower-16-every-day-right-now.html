Every Day or Right Now?

Some things happen every day, like a calendar 📅. Some things are happening right now, like a live camera 🎥. Pick the right words!

📅 EVERY DAY
🚶‍♀️

Calendar or camera?

Look for the clue words.

📅

Every day? It happens again and again. Use the doing word on its own.

Every day, Ravi walks to school.

🎥

Right now? It is happening as you look. Use am, is or are + -ing.

Look! Ravi is walking to school.

Watch out!

Two little bits that go missing.

The missing -s

He, she, it or one name, every day? Add -s!

He walk to school. → He walks to school.

Mei play the piano. → Mei plays the piano.

The missing helper or -ing

Right now needs both: is + -ing.

She walking. → She is walking.

She is walk. → She is walking.

Who is it?

The person decides the ending.

📅 Every day

he, she, it, one name: add -s

She walks. The cat sleeps.

I, you, we, they, two or more: no -s

I walk. They play.

  • every day
  • every morning
  • on Sundays
  • always
  • usually

🎥 Right now

I → am + -ing

he, she, it, one name → is + -ing

you, we, they, two or more → are + -ing

I am eating. She is singing. They are playing.

  • now
  • right now
  • Look!
  • Listen!
  • Shh!

Tricky endings

Some doing words change a bit, just like naming words in Lesson 9.

Add -es

Ends in s, x, ch, sh or o?

  • watch → watches
  • wash → washes
  • fix → fixes
  • go → goes

y → ies

No vowel before the y? Change it to ies.

  • cry → cries
  • fly → flies
  • carry → carries

A vowel before the y? Just -s: play → plays.
