Watch out!

The word and the thing must match: one with one, more with more.

"This" and "that" are for one. "These" and "those" are for more than one.

this shoes → these shoes 👟👟

those book → that book 📕

The four words

Two near words, two far words.

this

near · one

This apple in my hand is red. 🍎

I am holding this book.

these

near · more than one

These sweets in my hand are yummy. 🍬

I am wearing these shoes.

that

far · one

Look at that bird up in the sky! 🐦

That house over there is big.

those

far · more than one

Those clouds over there are dark. ☁️

Who are those children over there?

Two more things

Good to know.

is or are?

  • this is · that is
  • these are · those are

These is my toys.
These are my toys.

those, not them

Never put "them" in front of a thing. Say those.

Look at them birds!
Look at those birds!
