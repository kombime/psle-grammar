Three Steps Up

How many are you looking at?

Count, then pick the step.

1

One thing? Use the plain describing word. It tells you what something is like.

Ravi is tall. · a red ball · a hot drink

2

Two things? Add -er and use than.

Ravi is taller than Mei.

3

Three or more? Add -est and use the.

Ali is the tallest boy in the class.

Watch out!

Only one way up the stairs.

Use -er or more, never both. Use -est or most, never both!

more taller → taller

the most biggest → the biggest

Spelling changes

ends in…23
e: nicenicernicest
y: happyhappierhappiest
big, hot, fatbiggerhottest

Ends in e? Just add r or st. Ends in y? Change it to i. Big, hot, fat, sad: double the last letter.

Long words: more, most

more beautiful · the most beautiful

more colourful · the most delicious

Long words like beautiful, colourful, delicious and expensive don't take -er or -est.

Surprise stairs

Two words change completely, and naming words can describe too.

Learn these by heart

  • good → better → best 🏆
  • bad → worse → worst 🌧️

gooder, baddest are not real words.

The school hall

A naming word can describe another naming word. It tells you what kind.

  • the school hall
  • a birthday cake
  • the bus stop

It never gets -s: my school bag.
