Rocket to Tomorrow

To talk about later, hop on a rocket: will or going to. The doing word rides along plain, with no -s, -ed or -ing. Fix the sentence to blast off!

TOMORROW ✨🚀

Getting ready to launch

Look for the later words: tomorrow, tonight, soon, next week, next year.

1

Is it later? Then you need a rocket: will, or am / is / are going to.

Tomorrow, I will swim. · Tonight, I am going to swim.

2

Use the plain doing word. Leave -s, -ed and -ing on the ground!

swimsswamswimming

Watch out!

Things that stop the rocket.

Extra bits on the doing word, or a missing helper.

He will goes. → He will go.

She is going to swims. → She is going to swim.

I going to read. → I am going to read.

Three rockets

Each one takes you to later.

🚀 will

The same for everyone: I, you, he, she, we, they.

I will help you.

It will rain later.

🛸 am / is / are going to

I am · he, she, it, one name is · you, we, they are

I am going to fly a kite.

Mei is going to swim.

They are going to sing.

🛰️ Shall I? Shall we?

Use shall with I or we to ask, or to offer help.

Shall we play a game?

Shall I carry your bag?

Good to know

Two more things.

won't = will not

Remember the hug words from Lesson 5? Won't is short for will not.

I won't forget. · He won't come.

Before or later?

  • Yesterday, we went. ⏪
  • Tomorrow, we will go. 🚀
  • Last week, I was sick.
  • Next week, I will be eight.
