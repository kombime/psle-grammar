These words tell us more about the doing word.

?

How did it happen? Many how words end in -ly.

The tortoise walks slowly. 🐢

?

When did it happen?

I went to the zoo yesterday.

?

Where did it happen?

The children played outside.

Watch out!

How words that break the -ly rule.

Fast and hard stay the same

There is no "fastly".

Ravi runs fastly. → Ravi runs fast.

Mei works hard. 💪

Good becomes well

To say how someone does something, use well.

Siti sings good. → Siti sings well. 🎶

Don't forget the -ly

Ali shouted loud. → Ali shouted loudly.

The reporter's notebook

Words that answer each question.

How?

  • slowly
  • quickly
  • softly
  • loudly
  • carefully
  • fast · hard · well

When?

  • today · yesterday
  • tomorrow · soon
  • always · never
  • sometimes
  • early · every day

Where?

  • here · there
  • outside · inside
  • indoors
  • upstairs · downstairs
  • away · everywhere

More reporter tips

Spelling, and comparing two people.

y → ily

Ends in y? Change it to i, then add -ly.

  • happy → happily
  • easy → easily
  • sleepy → sleepily
  • noisy → noisily

Who did it better?

Short words add -er: fast → faster, hard → harder, early → earlier.

-ly words use more: more slowly, more carefully.
