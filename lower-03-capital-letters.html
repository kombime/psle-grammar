Crown Letters

A capital letter is a crown letter. Only special words get to wear a crown!

Tap every word that needs a crown.

Two questions

Ask these about each word.

1

Is it the first word of the sentence? Is it the word I?

Yes? Give it a crown: We play. Mum and I swim.

2

Is it a special name? A person, a day, a month or a place?

Yes? Crown it: Mei, Monday, June, Singapore.

Ordinary words stay small

A word for any person, place or thing is not special. It stays small.
