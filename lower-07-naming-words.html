Name Tags

Every person, animal, place and thing has a naming word, like boy, cat or park. Some have their very own special name too, like Lina, Fluffy or Bishan!

Tap every naming word.

Two questions

Ask these about a word.

1

Does it name a person, an animal, a place or a thing? Then it is a naming word.

🧒 boy · 🦊 fox · 🏫 school · 🖊️ pen

2

Is it the name of one special person, pet or place? Then it is a special name, and it starts with a capital letter.

Lina · Fluffy · Bishan

Not every word is a naming word

Words for what you do are not naming words. Words that describe are not naming words either.

Doing words

  • sit
  • eat
  • sing
  • write

Describing words

  • big
  • red
  • happy
  • soft

The boy has a red pen.

Only boy and pen are naming words.

Special names always start with a capital letter.

Days and months have names too

📅 Sunday, March

Each day and month has its own special name. So they start with a capital letter.

Sunday, Monday, March, June

But day, week and month are just naming words.
