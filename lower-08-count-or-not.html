One, Two or a Carton?

At the mini mart, some things you can count: one egg, two eggs. Some things you can't count one by one, like milk. They come in a pack: a carton of milk!

Tap something on the shelf.

One question

Ask: can I count it, one, two, three?

🔢

Yes? For more than one, add -s.

one apple → three apples

📦

No? Never add -s. It stays the same.

rice · water · sugar · milk

No -s on can't-count words

You can't count rice grain by grain, so it never gets an -s.

I ate some rices. → I ate some rice.

Mum bought some breads. → Mum bought some bread.

A few, a little, a lot of

Which words go with which things?

a few 🍪

For things you can count.

a few cookies · a few books

a little 🥛

For things you can't count.

a little milk · a little sugar

a lot of 🎉

For both kinds!

a lot of toys · a lot of water

Pack words and pair words

We can count the packs!

📦 Pack words

  • a carton of milk / juice
  • a loaf of bread
  • a bowl of rice
  • a glass of water
  • a bar of soap

🧦 Pair words

  • a pair of socks
  • a pair of shoes
  • a pair of sunglasses
  • two pairs of spectacles

Things that come in twos use "pair". More than one pair? Say pairs.
