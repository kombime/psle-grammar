The Big Parade

In the parade, partners march two by two: a boy word and its girl word. Then whole groups march together, under one banner!

Two kinds of words

Some words come in pairs. Some words are for a whole group.

👫

Partner words. Many boy words have a girl word partner.

king → queen · father → mother

🚩

Group words. Lots of the same thing together get a group word: a ___ of ___.

a flock of birds · a bunch of flowers

Watch out!

Two mix-ups that happen a lot.

Nephew or niece?

Your brother's or sister's son is your nephew. Their daughter is your niece.

Mei is my nephew. → Mei is my niece. 👧

Arjun is my niece. → Arjun is my nephew. 👦

Each animal has its own group

Cows don't fly, so they are not a flock!

a flock of cows → a herd of cows

a herd of bees → a swarm of bees

Who marches in the parade?

Partners first, then the groups.

Family partners

Boy word first, then the girl word.

  • boy · girl
  • man · woman
  • father · mother
  • son · daughter
  • brother · sister
  • uncle · aunt
  • nephew · niece
  • husband · wife
  • grandfather · grandmother

Royal and animal partners

Some girl words just add -ess.

  • king · queen 👑
  • rooster · hen 🐔
  • prince · princess
  • lion · lioness
  • waiter · waitress
  • actor · actress

Look: waiter and actor lose a letter. Princess has two s's.

More about groups

Two things to remember.

One banner, many things

  • a bunch of flowers, grapes, bananas, keys
  • a flock of birds, sheep
  • a herd of cows, elephants

A school of fish is a group of fish. No teachers! 🐟

After "of": more than one

  • a flock of bird → birds
  • a herd of cow → cows
  • a flock of sheeps → sheep

A group has lots of them, so use the "more than one" word. Remember: sheep stays sheep!
