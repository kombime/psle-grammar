Sports Day Houses

On Sports Day, everyone runs for a house. The house picks the helper words: Team One, Team Many, or Team I!

1. Tap a runner. 2. Send them to their house.

🏁

Which house?

Ask: who is it? Then pick from that house.

1

One? He, she, it, one name, one thing: Team One.

Ravi is · the cat has

2

More than one? We, they, two names, many things: Team Many.

they are · the cats have

3

Is it I? I has its own house!

I am · I was · I have · I do

Watch out!

Runners in the wrong house.

Check who it is before you pick the word.

I is ready. → I am ready.

They was late. → They were late.

He have a cold. → He has a cold.

Four races

Each race has a Team One word and a Team Many word.

is · are · am

Now, today.

Ravi is tall.

The cats are asleep.

I am seven.

was · were

Before: yesterday, last week.

She was tired.

They were late.

I was sick.

has · have

For things you own or get.

My sister has long hair.

We have two dogs.

I have a bag.

does · do

For asking, and for doing jobs.

Does he like durian?

Do they live here?

I do my homework.

Special runners

Two runners who surprise people.

🫵 You is always Team Many

Even when "you" is one person!

  • you are
  • you were
  • you have · do you…?

You was right. → You were right.

👭 Two names with "and"

Ravi and Mei are two people, so they run for Team Many.

  • Ravi and Mei are friends.
  • Mum and Dad were at work.
  • Arjun and I are seven.
