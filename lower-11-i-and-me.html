Throw and Catch

Some words throw: they do the action. Some words catch: the action comes to them. Help the ball fly!

🧒thrower
⚾
👧catcher

Thrower or catcher?

Ask: is this person doing it, or getting it?

🧒

Doing it? Use a thrower word. It comes before the doing word.

I throw · he runs · she sings · we play · they eat

👧

Getting it? Use a catcher word. It comes after the doing word, or after to, for, with, at.

hug me · to him · for her · with us · at them

Watch out!

The tricky one: two people together.

Cover the friend!

When there are two people, cover the friend's name. Then listen.

Mei and me went… ❌  Mei and I went… ✅

Mei and me went to the zoo.
→ Mei and I went to the zoo.

It works for catchers too

Cover the friend again. Which word sounds right?

Mum took Mei and I… ❌  Mum took Mei and me… ✅

Mum took Mei and I to the zoo.
→ Mum took Mei and me to the zoo.

Five teams

Each thrower word has its own catcher.

I · me

I like rice.

Mum hugs me.

he · him

He is tall.

I wave to him.

she · her

She can swim.

I give her a card.

we · us

We are friends.

Grandma cooks for us.

they · them

They play football.

I play with them.

Good to know

Two more things.

you and it never change

You can throw and catch: You help me. I help you.

It can too: It is big. I like it.

Friend first, it's polite!

Put the other person first, and yourself last.

Mei and I made a card. 💌
Mum hugged Mei and me.
