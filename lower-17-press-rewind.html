Two questions

Look for the rewind words: yesterday, last night, last week, just now, two days ago.

⏪

Did it already happen? Use a past word. Most add -ed. Some are surprise words.

walk → walked · eat → ate

⏸️

Was it in the middle of happening? Use was or were + -ing.

I was eating when the phone rang. 📞

Watch out!

Surprise words never take -ed.

These words don't exist! Use the surprise word instead.

eated → ate · goed → went

buyed → bought · runned → ran

catched → caught · taked → took

Three kinds of rewind

Add -ed, learn the surprise words, or use was and were.

Add -ed

walk → walked · play → played

jump → jumped · help → helped

Surprise past words

These change in their own way. Learn them by heart!

  • eat → ate
  • go → went
  • see → saw
  • run → ran
  • come → came
  • sit → sat
  • drink → drank
  • swim → swam
  • sing → sang
  • write → wrote
  • make → made
  • take → took
  • give → gave
  • buy → bought
  • catch → caught
  • sleep → slept
  • have → had

was / were + -ing

In the middle of doing something, when something else happened.

I / he / she / it → was eating

we / you / they → were eating

We were playing when it rained. 🌧️

Tricky -ed endings

Look at the end of the doing word first.

Ends in e? Just add d

  • bake → baked
  • smile → smiled

y → ied

No vowel before the y? Change it to ied.

  • cry → cried
  • carry → carried
  • try → tried

A vowel before the y? Just add ed: play → played, stay → stayed.
