Comma Fences

A comma , is a little fence. It keeps each thing in a list in its own space.

Tap the gaps that need a fence.

Two steps for a list

A list is three or more things.

1

Put a comma fence between the things.

apples, oranges, mangoes …

2

Before the last thing, just say and.

apples, oranges, mangoes and pears.

Don't forget the fences

Without fences, the things all bump into each other.

I like cats dogs and fish.

I like cats, dogs and fish.

Three places for a comma

🧺 In a list

Between the things, with and before the last one.

We saw lions, tigers and bears.

I need a pen, a ruler and a book.

👍 After Yes or No

Put a comma after "Yes" or "No" at the start.

Yes, you can.

No, thank you.

💬 Before speech marks

Put a comma after "said" or "asked", before someone talks.

Jane said, "I like flowers."

Tom asked, "Can I play?"

Only two things?

✌️ No fence needed

With just two things, and is enough. No comma!

cats, and dogs → cats and dogs

bread and butter
